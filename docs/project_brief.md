# Project Brief: Repeat Purchase Recovery

## Background

Customer acquisition costs continue to rise across e-commerce and retail verticals. Retaining existing customers and converting one-time buyers into repeat purchasers is a high-leverage growth lever. This project establishes a data-driven framework to identify, understand, and recover lapsed or at-risk repeat buyers.

---

## Business Objective

Identify the root causes of low repeat purchase rates, quantify the revenue impact, and produce actionable recommendations that a product or growth team can execute within one quarter.

---

## Success Metrics

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| 90-Day Repeat Rate | Measure current baseline; define uplift goal after analysis |
| Median Time to 2nd Purchase | Measure current baseline |
| Churn Identification Accuracy | Define precision target after model training |
| Revenue from Recovered Customers | Quantify opportunity after analysis |

---

## Scope

- **In scope:** Order-level transaction data, customer demographics (if available), product/category data, channel data
- **Out of scope:** Real-time intervention infrastructure, A/B test design (recommendations only), marketing spend data
